How It Works
Goals

Goals are the big achievements you're working towards. They can be in any area of your life:

  • Physical Fitness
  • Professional Development
  • Learning
  • Relationships
  • Personal Projects

Each goal has a category, description, deadline, and priority level.

Routines

Routines help you structure your day for maximum productivity and well-being:

  • Create time blocks for different activities
  • Assign energy levels (High, Medium, Low)
  • Schedule activities for specific days of the week
  • Balance work, rest, and personal time
Habits

Habits are the daily actions that move you toward your goals:

  • Link habits to specific goals
  • Track your daily completion
  • Build streaks for consistency
  • View your progress over time
Analytics

The analytics dashboard helps you understand your progress:

  • See habit completion rates
  • Track your current streaks
  • Analyze your energy distribution
  • Identify patterns and areas for improvement
